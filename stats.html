The story of Artemiy Alexandrovich Glavatskyh (Главатских, Артемий Александрович):

While checking for duplicates in the data table, a soldier with the same name was found listed twice: once as a POW in March 2022, and again as KIA in July 2024. A quick online search (including photos) confirmed the entries referred to the same person.

Artemiy Alexandrovich Glavatskyh was born in 1998 in Udmurtia, Russia. His first mentions as a POW can be found on the fifth day of the Russian invasion of Ukraine (28 February 2022) and in the days that followed: https://poligon.media/poligon-verificziroval-lichnosti-soldat/ https://t.me/svoiua/275 https://www.svoboda.org/a/nadeemsya-chto-on-vernyotsya-rossiyskie-plennye-kotoryh-net/31744461.html
